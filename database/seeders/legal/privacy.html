2. What we collect, and why

Most of what we hold comes from you. The rest is created as you use the site, such as your sign-in sessions and your orders, or comes from a sign-in service or a payment provider that you chose to use.

Your account

Nickname, email address, password (we keep only a scrambled version of it, never the password itself), time zone, language, and, if you turn it on, your two-factor secret and recovery codes.

Why we use it: to make your account, sign you in and keep it secure. (Contract)

Your profile

Avatar, bio, forum signature and social links if you add them, and your activity, such as your posts, reputation and badges.

Why we use it: to show your profile and run the community. (Contract)

Signing in with another service

If you sign in with Google, Discord or Steam, the identifier and the profile details that service shares with us, for example your name and email address.

Why we use it: to let you sign in that way. (Contract)

What you post

Forum threads and replies, blog comments, reviews, poll votes, support tickets and their attachments, and private messages.

Why we use it: to run the forum, blog, shop and support, and to keep the community safe. (Contract and legitimate interests)

Orders and billing

What you bought, prices and tax, coupons or gift cards you used, your subscriptions and their status, the licence keys and access you hold, your billing and shipping addresses, the type of card and its last four digits, and the identifiers that our payment providers give us. We do not receive or keep your full card number.

Why we use it: to take payment, deliver what you bought, send invoices, give refunds and keep the records that the law requires. (Contract and legal obligation)

API keys and usage

The keys you make (kept in a form that cannot be read back), their scopes and when they were last used, and a count of the requests made with them each month.

Why we use it: to make your keys work and to apply limits. (Contract)

Technical data

Your IP address, your browser and device details, and the times of your visits, kept with your sign-in sessions and in security logs. For staff, the admin audit log also records what they changed and from where.

Why we use it: to keep the site secure, stop abuse and fix problems. (Legitimate interests)

Searches

The words searched for on the site and how many results there were. They are not linked to your account or your address.

Why we use it: to make search better. (Legitimate interests)

Messages we send you

Your email address, whether an email was delivered, and your choices about notifications and email digests.

Why we use it: to send account, security and order emails, and the notifications you ask for. (Contract and consent)

Moderation records

Reports, warnings, bans and the reasons for them, and what automatic rules, such as spam filters, did.

Why we use it: to enforce the rules and protect members. (Legitimate interests)

Cookies and browser storage

See our cookie policy.

3. Who we share it with

We do not sell your personal information. We share it only with:

  • Service providers who work for us. [Hosting provider] (hosting and storage), [Email provider] (sending our emails), Stripe, and Tebex where the shop sells through it (payments), and Pusher (delivering live notifications to your browser). They may use the information only to provide their service to us.
  • Services you choose to connect. Google, Discord or Steam, if you sign in with them, and Discord, if you link your account to get roles or notifications there.
  • Other members and the public. Your nickname, avatar, profile and what you post in public places can be seen by everyone, including search engines. A private message can be seen by the people in that conversation. Our staff can see what their role allows, and use it only to run the site.
  • Authorities and others where required. Where the law requires it, to protect people's safety or our rights, or if the business is sold or reorganised, in which case we will tell you.

At the time of writing, the site does not use advertising or analytics trackers. If that changes, this policy will be updated first, and the cookie banner will ask you before anything optional is turned on.

4. Moving information abroad

Some of the providers above are in other countries, or use servers there, including outside the country where you live. Where that is the case, we make sure your information is protected in the way the law requires, for example by using standard contract terms approved for that purpose, or a provider that is covered by an approved framework.

6. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have rights over your information. You can:

  • See it and take a copy. Privacy settings lets you ask for an export of your information. We prepare a ZIP file (with JSON and CSV files in it) and email you when it is ready to download.
  • Correct it. Change your details and profile in your settings, or ask us.
  • Have it erased. Ask on the same privacy settings page. A member of our team deals with the request and confirms when it is done, within 30 days.
  • Object, or ask us to limit how we use it, and take back a consent you gave, by writing to [Contact email].
  • Ask a person to look again at a decision that an automatic rule made about your content.
  • Complain to the data protection authority where you live or work. We would like the chance to put things right first.

We may ask you to prove who you are before we act on a request, so that we do not give your information to someone else.

7. What erasing your account covers

When we erase your account, we remove the personal information we hold about you, apart from what we have to keep or what is needed to protect others. That can include records of orders and payments (see section 5), moderation records needed to enforce a ban, and content that other members have quoted in their own posts, which belongs to their conversation. A person handles each request, so we can tell you what was removed and what we kept, and why.

8. Children

The site is not meant for anyone under [Minimum age]. If we learn that someone younger has an account, we will close it and erase their information. If you think this has happened, please contact us.

9. Keeping your information safe

We protect your information with measures that fit the risk. Connections to the site are encrypted, passwords are stored as scrambled hashes, you can turn on two-factor sign-in, staff access depends on role, and what staff change in the admin area is logged. No system is perfectly secure. If you find a weakness, please tell us through our security contact. If a breach puts your information at risk, we will tell you and the authorities as the law requires.
