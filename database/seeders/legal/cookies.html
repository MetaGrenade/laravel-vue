This policy explains the cookies and similar storage that {{site_name}} uses, why, and how long they last. It is part of our privacy policy.

1. What cookies are

A cookie is a small text file that a website saves in your browser. The site also uses "local storage", which is similar: a small amount of data kept in your browser. In this policy, "cookies" means both.

2. What we use

Necessary cookies

These keep you signed in and keep the site working. They are always on, and the site cannot work without them.

  • {{session_cookie}} keeps you signed in and remembers things between pages, such as the message that confirms you saved something. It is set when you visit the site, even if you do not sign in. It lasts {{session_minutes}} minutes after your last visit.
  • XSRF-TOKEN stops other websites from sending forms to this site as if they were you. It lasts {{session_minutes}} minutes after your last visit.
  • remember_web_… keeps you signed in on this device if you tick "Remember me" when you sign in. It is not set unless you do. It lasts up to {{remember_days}} days.
  • cookie_consent remembers the choice you made on the cookie banner. It lasts {{consent_days}} days.

Preference cookies

These remember a choice you make on the site. They are set only when you change the setting, and they are not used to track you.

  • appearance remembers whether you chose the light, dark or system theme. A copy is kept in local storage under the same name. It lasts {{appearance_days}} days.
  • sidebar_state remembers whether the side menu is open or closed. A copy is kept in local storage as "sidebar". It lasts {{sidebar_days}} days.

The editor also keeps a draft of what you are writing in your browser's local storage, so that you do not lose it if the page closes. It stays on your device until you post it or clear your browser's data. It is not sent to us until you post.

Optional cookies

These are cookies for things such as analytics or advertising. At the time of writing, the site does not use any. If that changes, they will stay off until you choose "Allow all" on the cookie banner, and this page will say what they are.

3. Cookies from other services

Some pages load things from other companies. They may set their own cookies or similar identifiers, which we do not control:

  • Stripe loads on pages where you pay, to take the payment securely and to help prevent fraud.
  • Tebex, if the shop sells through it, runs the checkout on its own pages, under its own policy.
  • Google, Discord and Steam are used when you choose to sign in with them.
  • Pusher delivers live notifications to your browser, and receives your IP address when your browser connects.

Paying is not optional, so these do not wait for the cookie banner. Please read their own policies for the details.

4. Your choices

  • The cookie banner is at the bottom of the page until you choose. "Necessary only" keeps the necessary cookies above. "Allow all" is the only choice that turns optional cookies on. Your choice lasts {{consent_days}} days.
  • To change your choice, delete the cookie_consent cookie in your browser's settings and reload the page. The banner comes back.
  • You can also block or delete cookies in your browser's settings. If you block the necessary cookies, you will not be able to sign in.
