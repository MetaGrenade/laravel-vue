# What is in MetaForger

What the boilerplate gives you out of the box, module by module, and the technology it is built on. The [README](../README.md) is the short version; each module here links to the page that explains it properly.

> **Status: pre-1.0.** The shop works end to end (catalogue, cart, discount codes and gift cards, shipping and tax, Stripe checkout, orders, refunds, digital downloads and licence keys, wishlists and reviews). Tebex purchases are a preview that has not been checked against a real store. Subscription plans give entitlements (a feature, a limit). The road to a stable 1.0.0 is in the [roadmap](ROADMAP-v1.0.0.md), and each release's changes are in the [CHANGELOG](../CHANGELOG.md).

## The stack

- **Backend – Laravel 13 (PHP 8.4+)** with Sanctum for API tokens, Spatie Permissions for RBAC, Laravel Cashier (Stripe) for subscriptions, a provider-neutral payment layer for the shop, queue/listener scaffolding, and opinionated seeders for fast iteration.
- **Frontend – Vue 3 + Inertia.js 3 + TypeScript** with Ziggy-powered routing, optional server-side rendering, and theme initialization in a single SPA shell.
- **UI & Editor Toolkit** – Tailwind CSS 4, shadcn-vue components on Reka UI, Lucide icons, Vue Sonner toasts, and Tiptap 3 rich text editing for forum and blog content.
- **Data Visualization** – Unovis charts for dashboards inside the admin area.
- **Payments** – Stripe Checkout for shop orders and Cashier for subscriptions, both behind idempotent webhooks (`App\Payments`). Tebex plugs in behind the same `PaymentProvider` contract, as a preview ([payments.md](payments.md), [tebex.md](tebex.md)).
- **Developer Experience** – Vite 8 (Rolldown), ESLint 10 + Prettier, Pint, Larastan (PHPStan level 5), PHPUnit 12, a CI matrix that runs the tests on SQLite, MySQL 8 and PostgreSQL 16, and convenience scripts for running Laravel, queues, SSR, and Vite together.
- **Secure & search-friendly by default** – HTML sanitisation for user content, rate limiting, security headers with a nonce-based CSP, per-page SEO metadata, structured data, an XML sitemap and a dynamic `robots.txt` ([security.md](security.md), [seo.md](seo.md)).
- **Ready for more languages** – English only for now, but interface strings, locale selection and a completeness check (`php artisan lang:check`) are in place ([i18n.md](i18n.md), [adding-a-language.md](adding-a-language.md)).

## Modules

- **Forum System**: Boards, threads, post moderation, publishing workflows, and tracking read state with dedicated controllers and routes.
- **Blog & Previewing**: Public blog listing, tokenized preview links, and authenticated commenting APIs.
- **Support Center**: Ticket submission, messaging threads, authenticated access to customer conversations, and configurable
  assignment rules so tickets auto-route to the right agents or support teams without touching the database, and service levels that raise the priority of a ticket nobody has answered or hand it to someone else ([support-assignment.md](support-assignment.md)). Attachments are private and only served through an authorised download route ([support-attachments.md](support-attachments.md)).
- **Shop** (usable end to end; still being completed, see [commerce.md](commerce.md) for how it works and [payments.md](payments.md) for taking money):
  - **Catalogue**: products with options and variants, one active price in the store currency, brands, categories and tags, and stock tracked in a movement ledger with manual adjustments and low-stock warnings. A product can be free, untaxed, or not need shipping.
  - **Pictures & storefront**: several pictures per product (re-encoded to WebP at three sizes), a catalogue with prices and savings, and a product page with a gallery, option pickers that follow the variants, stock described in words (never counts) and structured data.
  - **Cart & discount codes**: guest and signed-in carts (a guest's cart and orders follow them into their account when they sign in and verify their email); percentage, fixed-amount and free-shipping codes with dates, a minimum spend, usage limits and product or category restrictions.
  - **Checkout**: guest or signed-in, an address book (Settings → Addresses), shipping zones and rates, a tax-rate table and a live quote as the shopper types. Payment happens on hosted Stripe Checkout; stock is held for an unpaid order and released when it expires. Orders that cost nothing are paid on the spot without a provider.
  - **Orders & refunds**: an order lifecycle with an append-only history, receipts and shipping emails, fulfilment with tracking, cancellation, notes, and full or partial refunds through Stripe (its own permission, `commerce.acp.refund`).
  - **Digital goods**: files kept on a private disk, per-order download grants, short-lived signed links, download limits and optional expiry, revoked automatically by a full refund. **Licence keys** are sold from an encrypted pool tied to the stock count (so a key is never sold twice), handed over when the order is paid and hidden again by a full refund.
  - **Gift cards**: staff issue stored value (the code is shown once and never kept, only its hash and last four characters), customers spend it at checkout on the whole order, shipping and tax included, one card per order. An order the card pays in full needs no payment provider, the card's share is given back if the order is not paid, and refunds go back onto the card first. A ledger records every change to a balance.
  - **Wishlists & reviews**: customers save products to a private wishlist and review what they bought (1 to 5 stars, plain text, "Verified purchase" for buyers); staff choose which reviews wait for approval and publish, reject or delete them in the ACP. Ratings show on product cards, the product page and its structured data.
  - **Tebex (a preview)**: connect a Tebex store with its public token and the shop keeps a local copy of its categories and packages (`php artisan tebex:sync`, on a schedule, or from the ACP), shows them as its own storefront, holds them in the cart and, at checkout, makes a basket at Tebex and sends the buyer there. It sells for the games as well as for universal stores: a Minecraft or Overwolf buyer types their username, and a Discord, FiveM, Rust (and other Steam-based game) buyer signs in through Tebex before paying. Tebex works out the tax and takes its own codes and gift cards, and tells the shop by webhook when a payment is made, refunded or disputed (set `TEBEX_WEBHOOK_SECRET`). Buyers pay on Tebex's own page, or with `TEBEX_CHECKOUT_MODE=popup` or `inline` on a page of the shop's with Tebex.js. A recurring package's renewals are recorded as payments and where the recurring payment stands is kept and shown (Tebex charges and ends it). What a purchase gives the buyer's account (a feature, a limit) is set per package, given when the order is paid and taken back with a refund, a lost dispute or the end of a recurring payment ([entitlements.md](entitlements.md)). An **experimental** mode (`TEBEX_CHECKOUT_API_ENABLED`, for stores Tebex has approved for its Checkout API) sells the shop's own products through Tebex instead; it refunds through the API too. **Not finished:** it has not been checked against a real store, so treat it as a preview until it has. See [tebex.md](tebex.md).
  - **Admin**: Commerce, Products, Orders, Reviews, Brands and tags, Shipping, Tax rates, Discount codes, Gift cards and Tebex in the ACP.
  - **Not built yet**: selling gift cards as a product, and entitlements from the shop's own products (on the roadmap; Stripe plans give them already).
- **Polls & Surveys**: Polls with admin management, voting and an API.
- **Global Search**: One search across blog posts, forum threads and FAQs with admin analytics. The default searches the database; with `SEARCH_DRIVER=meilisearch` the page reads the index (settings via `php artisan metaforger:search:sync`, documents via `php artisan metaforger:search:reindex`). The index only keeps what a guest can already open. **Admin → System settings** and `php artisan metaforger:search:health` say whether Meilisearch is reachable and holds what it should, and a CI job runs the search tests against a real Meilisearch ([search-index.md](search-index.md)).
- **Billing & Subscriptions**: Stripe-powered subscriptions via Laravel Cashier, an end-user settings page for plan management, and an admin invoice browser with webhook visibility. Plans give entitlements, and a failed renewal is followed up: the customer is emailed once for each attempt (and when it goes through, or the subscription ends), and can press **Pay now** after fixing their card. A plan can give a new customer a free trial, with a reminder before it ends, and a customer can enter a Stripe promotion code that is checked before they pay, and the billing page links to Stripe's customer portal ([billing.md](billing.md)). A metered-usage example counts each account's API calls per month against what its plan allows, with a usage panel for customers and a report for staff ([usage.md](usage.md)).
- **Admin Control Panel (ACP)**: Inertia-powered layouts under `resources/js/pages/acp` for managing users, forums, support assignment rules, team membership, the shop, and content. Permission middleware ensures only privileged roles can reach moderation endpoints.
- **Admin audit log**: every change made in the admin area is written to an append-only log (who, what it was done to, what changed as it was and as it became, where from, and whether it worked), with a viewer in **Admin → Audit log**, a view of everything done on one account, and a retention setting ([audit-log.md](audit-log.md)).
- **Read-only impersonation**: staff can see the site as one customer sees it, to help with a support case. It needs a reason, lasts 30 minutes, cannot change anything (and does not even keep what looking changes), keeps secrets such as recovery codes and downloads out of view, and writes every page opened to the audit log ([impersonation.md](impersonation.md)).
- **Branded emails**: every email the site sends is in one layout with the site's name and colour (or a logo), a footer, a plain-text version and a dark one. Set `MAIL_BRAND_COLOR`, `MAIL_LOGO_URL` and `MAIL_FOOTER`; the words on buttons and the colour of links are chosen so they can be read on any colour ([email.md](email.md)).
- **"Get started" checklist**: a card on a new customer's dashboard lists what to do first (confirm the email, add a picture or bio, turn on two-factor, choose notifications, choose a plan, make an API key, say hello in the forum). Each step ticks itself from the account, and the card goes when everything is done, when it is hidden, or after 30 days ([onboarding.md](onboarding.md)).
- **Warnings and bans**: a moderator can warn a member, ban them for good or until a date, and lift a ban, all with a reason; each is kept in the member's history, they are emailed and can read it in their account, and a suspension lifts itself when it ends. A warning is only a notice: nothing happens automatically ([moderation.md](moderation.md)).
- **Automod and a review queue for the forum**: administrators set rules (phrases, too many links or a blocked domain, the same text again, too many posts too fast, for everybody or only new accounts) and a post that breaks one is held out of sight until a moderator approves it in a queue, or refused. Held posts tell nobody and earn nothing until approved ([automod.md](automod.md)).
- **Clearing out a spammer**: a moderator takes down everything a member posted in one go (threads unpublished, replies deleted, blog comments and product reviews rejected, what is waiting in the review queue rejected), on its own, together with a ban, or for several members at once from the Users list. Nothing is destroyed for good, the member is not told, and it is written to their history ([moderation.md](moderation.md)).
- **Authentication & Authorization**: Starter-kit authentication (registration, login, password reset, email verification, TOTP two-factor and social login) plus Spatie role/permission gating surfaced to the SPA via dedicated composables ([two-factor.md](two-factor.md), [social-login.md](social-login.md), [acp-permissions.md](acp-permissions.md)).
- **HTTP API**: a versioned API under `/api/v1` with keys that carry scopes, interactive documentation and a generated OpenAPI schema ([api.md](api.md), [api-keys.md](api-keys.md)).
- **Live updates**: notifications and new forum replies pushed to an open browser with Pusher, when it is set up ([realtime.md](realtime.md)).
- **Customer Dashboard**: after sign-in, a customer sees their newest orders and a shortcut to the order pages where their licence keys and downloads are found (when the shop is enabled), their plan, their account security and shortcuts, plus their tickets, forum activity and recommended reading. Areas an administrator has switched off are left off it.
- **Appearance Management**: System/light/dark modes synced between SSR and the client through a reusable composable.

## Website sections and feature toggles

- **Module switches** live in the Admin Control Panel under **System Settings → Website Sections**. Operators can enable or disable the four major areas (the blog, the forum, the support desk and the shop) without touching code or editing configuration files.
- **Social login toggles** sit alongside those settings so administrators can enable or disable Google, Discord, or Steam OAuth flows in real time ([social-login.md](social-login.md)). Disabled providers disappear from the login and registration pages, and their redirect/callback routes automatically return `404` responses.
- **Consistent enforcement** is handled by the `EnsureWebsiteSectionIsEnabled` middleware and shared Inertia props. When a section is disabled the corresponding navigation links, global search providers, and HTTP routes automatically return `404` responses for visitors. (The Stripe webhook and a customer's receipt and download links stay reachable so payments in flight still settle.)
- **Safe defaults** ensure newly provisioned environments start with every section available while still honoring explicit `false` values saved in the database, so administrators always see the exact state they configured.
- **Adding your own section** that can be switched off the same way is a recipe in [extending.md](extending.md).
